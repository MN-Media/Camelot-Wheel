Camelot Wheel Pro

Harmonic Mixing Reference

The standard Camelot wheel gives you three compatibility types: same key, one step adjacent, and relative major/minor. Camelot Wheel Pro expands this to 8 directional match categories — letting you plan not just which keys mix, but how the energy of a set moves with each transition.

Why this wheel is different

Traditional harmonic mixing only answers "will these keys clash?" This wheel answers "where does this take the crowd?" Boost moves raise perceived energy and tension. Drop moves lower it. Each level quantifies how dramatic the shift is — so you can plan a slow burn build, a peak-time push, or a smooth comedown with precision.

The result: up to 8× more intentional transitions per key compared to a standard wheel, each with a defined energy direction.

Match Categories

Perfect Match Same harmonic centre, opposite mode (major ↔ minor). The smoothest possible transition — virtually no tonal shift, just a change in emotional colour.
Mood Change A specific jump that preserves harmonic compatibility while introducing a distinctly different emotional character — great for section breaks.
Boost Lv 1 One step up the circle of fifths. A mild, natural energy lift — the most common DJ technique for building momentum.
Drop Lv 1 One step down the circle. A gentle release of tension — works well after a peak or to ease into a deeper groove.
Boost Lv 2 A moderate upward energy shift. Still harmonically compatible, but with a noticeable increase in brightness and drive.
Drop Lv 2 A moderate downward shift. Noticeably cooler and more relaxed — use to signal a new phase in your set.
Boost Lv 3 Maximum energy boost. A bold, dramatic leap upward — creates tension and anticipation. Use sparingly for climactic moments.
Drop Lv 3 Maximum energy drop. A powerful comedown — ideal for closing a peak, introducing a break, or pivoting to a completely different vibe.

How to use

Select a key by clicking any segment on the wheel or any button in the Quick-Select grid. All compatible keys light up instantly in their category colour.

Read the panel (right side) for a grouped list of every compatible key, sorted by category. Click any chip to pivot to that key.

Filter categories using the slider icon in the settings bar to focus only on the match types relevant to your current goal.

Toggle Key Names (KN) to switch between Camelot notation (e.g. 8A) and musical key names (e.g. A min) — whichever matches your library tags.

Switch layouts with the view icons — including Dual Wheels: two independent wheels side by side, each with its own match-category settings below it. Ideal for comparing two scenarios at once.

Click the active key again to deselect and reset the wheel — or simply press R or Esc. Switch layout modes at any time without losing your selection.

Quick Check Mode

Two keys in. Verdict out.

Toggle Quick Check in the top bar — or press Q — to answer one question instantly: do these two keys work together? Pick a first key, then a second — the app immediately names the match category, or flags a Clash.

The first key stays locked while you tap through candidates — perfect for finding the right next track for the song you're playing. All 8 match categories count here, regardless of your filter settings.

Tap the locked key again, or press R / Esc, to reset.

Shortcuts

Q Toggle Quick Check mode on and off.
R Reset — clears the current selection: both keys in Quick Check, both wheels in the Dual view.
Esc Closes open dialogs (info, category filter) first — then resets, just like R.
